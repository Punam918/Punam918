## About

I'm an AI and Data Engineer focused on turning complex models and data workflows into reliable, production-ready systems.

My work spans large language models, high-precision retrieval-augmented generation, data engineering, MLOps, DevOps automation, and AI security. I enjoy building systems that are measurable, reproducible, and practical to operate.

**Current interests:** LLMs · RAG Systems · AI Agents · Data Platforms · MLOps · AI Security

## Research & Engineering

- **Language models:** Built a compact decoder-only Transformer from scratch, including tokenizer training, pretraining, evaluation, SFT, and DPO workflows.
- **RAG systems:** Design grounded retrieval pipelines with hybrid search, vector databases, reranking, and citation evidence.
- **AI engineering:** Build agentic workflows with LangChain, LangGraph, controlled tools, and human approval gates.
- **Data engineering:** Work with ingestion, validation, lakehouse publishing, vector indexing, and reproducible data workflows.
- **Production systems:** Apply MLOps, CI/CD, containerization, cloud deployment, and AI security practices to make systems reliable and maintainable.
